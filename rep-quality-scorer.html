On Start Set the app measures your rig's sensor noise while you hold still, then sets the motion threshold to 3× that (never below the minimum above). It keeps re-measuring during every pause, so it tunes itself to each machine — the dot-number beside noise σ counts those updates. No per-machine setup needed. Watch vert. accel peak during a rep — it must clearly beat eff. thresh or reps get missed. Guide at a 4 s tempo: 300 mm stroke peaks near 0.09 m/s² (comfortable), 150 mm near 0.046 (marginal), 100 mm near 0.031 (borderline). Shorter or slower needs a firmer mount or a quicker tempo.
