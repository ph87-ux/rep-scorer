The whole set is analysed when you press Stop, which lets it filter without lag and see each turnaround in context. Gravity and sensor noise are measured from your own recording — no per-machine setup.

Peak accel needs to be at least 3× threshold. Below that, phase times read short rather than reps being missed outright, which is the one failure you cannot spot by eye — so it is called out above the score. Acceleration goes as stroke ÷ time², so halving the tempo costs four times the signal: stroke in mm ≳ 10 × (phase seconds)² on a firm mount, roughly 16× on a loose one. At a 4 s tempo that is about 160 mm firm, 250 mm loose. Mount rigidity moves this more than anything else here. Pauses shorter than about 0.6 s cannot be separated from the strokes either side.
